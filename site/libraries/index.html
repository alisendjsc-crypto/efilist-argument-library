Reading the marks

The flagship sorts its objections into five tiers. Each tier has a mark: a small diagram of how an objection of that tier is built. It never pictures a topic.

Blocks are nodes. Thin lines are edges. A long bar underneath is a ground; corner pieces are brackets. One element is lit: look there first.

  1. T1 Emotional / Reflexivea reflex: the line goes out, turns, and comes back short of where it began
  2. T2 Folk Philosophicalhanded down: three nodes, each taken from the one above
  3. T3 Structural / Pragmatica structure: one node held up by two below it; take one away and it falls
  4. T4 Genuine Philosophicalbedrock: the reasons come down and stop on a primitive, which stays standing
  5. T5 Meta-Objectionabout the objections: a node held inside brackets

The library icons in your browser tab share this grammar: they name a library, where these five describe an objection.
