This library defends the right to choose abortion, against objections raised to it, in an optionality-only register — appraisal-silent, asymmetry-independent, importing no antinatalist premise — so the right stands for a pregnant person who rejects antinatalism outright. In Layer 1 it is not a claim that anyone should choose abortion; it is a defense of the option. The opening move is the bodily-sovereignty spine: a right to life is not a right to the use of an unwilling body, so granting fetal personhood arguendo does not overturn the default against bodily conscription. Early build (v0.1.0): the optionality spine — gestational self-determination is the default — is authored; the personhood-independence foundation, the responsibility and withdrawing-versus-killing defenses, and the remaining Layer-1 optionality defenses await authoring.
