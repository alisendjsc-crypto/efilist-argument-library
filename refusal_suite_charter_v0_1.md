## Umbrella

**Name: The Refusal Libraries** (working — Josiah's veto stands)
**Telos / tagline: suffering reduction**

The shared *form* is a refusal — each library is a "no" to something imposed or assumed: to creating suffering, to forced life, to imposed gestation, to making victims, to a non-consented natal assignment, to unconsented communion with humanity, to human supremacy. Every one is a negative posture toward something imposed; that is the genuine common structure, not a marketing gloss.

Suffering-reduction is the **motive** (tagline over both wings); refusal is the **structure** (the name). Suffering-reduction is *not* the name because it doesn't structurally cover Wing 2 — it would orphan the AI/misanthropy/bio-chauvinism cluster. Naming the stance keeps the second wing housed.

---

## Two wings

**Wing 1 — Operative / patient-facing**   (nav shorthand: *Harm & Autonomy*)
Acts *on* a subject's suffering or self-authorship: prevent the victim, end the suffering, free the chooser.
→ Antinatalism (flagship) · Right to Die · Abortion · Veganism · Anatomy Alteration

**Wing 2 — Attitudinal / category-facing**   (nav shorthand: *Anthropocentrism*)
Judges what stance is *fitting* toward humanity-as-such. Suffering is descriptive backdrop, never operative.
→ AI-as-Separation · Misanthropy Defense · Bio-Chauvinism Critique

**Boundary:** Wing 1 *reduces* suffering; Wing 2 *judges the thing that produces it.* The cut is operative-vs-attitudinal — **not** consent-vs-anthropocentrism. (Consent fails as the axis: suffering-reduction lives on both sides of a consent cut. The operative/attitudinal cut survives that, because Wing 2's relationship to suffering is descriptive only.)

## Strand-map (Wing 1 instrument only)

Wing 1 decomposes on four strands. Wing 2 runs on a separate *fittingness* axis, developed when Wing 2 is built — the four-strand map does **not** apply to it.

- **Harm** — operative on suffering (prevents victims / ends suffering).
- **Consent** — negative self-determination (don't impose X on me).
- **Sovereignty** — positive self-determination (I author my own X). *The "live free or die" strand: the positive face of the consent axis, NOT a third axis. Ground in universal self-ownership; keep the American/cultural framing as rhetorical color and RWE flavor, never the load-bearing frame — coding the claim as culturally American hands the objector "that's just your culture's value" for free.*

## Compensation — the suite primitive (load-bearing; keep its three forks distinct)

**1 — The upgrade: liberty → claim-right.**
Mainstream right-to-die is a *liberty* (let me die, the state steps aside). The compensational version is a *claim-right* (society **owes** the means, because it imposed the life). Strictly more demanding, far more efilist-consonant — and it is *why* the criminalization/isolation points bite. Under a liberty frame, criminalizing presence at a death is mere over-reach. Under compensation, it is the imposer *refusing to discharge a debt it incurred* — a compounding second wrong stacked on the original imposition. Forced isolation and excruciating methods aren't side-effects; they're the unpaid debt made visible.

**2 — Who owes it (gap to keep open).**
Imposition-debt, if it tracks the imposition, is owed by *procreators* — but the remedy can only come from the *state.* Different agents; "natalist society owes me a clean death" papers over the mismatch, and the sharp objector exploits it (*take it up with your parents*). Two repairs:
- (a) Society as **complicit** — pronatal incentive + valorization + criminalized exits = accessory, not bystander.
- (b) An **independent obstruction charge** — blocking a sovereign exit is wrong on *sovereignty alone*, no compensatory premise required.
→ **Lean on (b) as load-bearer.** It survives an objector who rejects the entire debt structure.

**3 — Universal vs conditional (fork to keep distinct).**
Compensation is owed to *everyone* (imposition is quality-independent, asymmetry-consonant — the wrong is in coming-to-be). Harm-relief is *triggered* by severe suffering. They pull opposite ways on *who gets access.* Use both — compensation as the floor, treatment-resistant cases as aggravators — but as **distinct response-lines**, never blurred:
- Universal/compensatory answers contagion + "you trivialize life." Audience: efilist-internal.
- Suffering-gated answers threshold-arbitrariness. Audience: broad-secular.
Pick per-objection which one you're standing on.

Compensation also appears in **Anatomy Alteration** (alteration compensates the non-consented natal assignment) → promoted to suite primitive. Building Right to Die also pilots the compensational bridge Anatomy will reuse.

## Register / scope invariant (added 2026-06-22, Josiah)

The suite defends the **optionality / sovereign right** to exercise contested choices (right to die, abortion, transgenderism) for deferring reasons. It **never argues any person should exercise them**; rebuttals defeat objections to the *right*, they do not exhort the *act*.

- **Abortion** is the lone exception permitting a positive but **strictly advisory** moral claim (antinatalist logic — aborting, incl. late-term, is probably better) with **no enforcement mechanism, ever**.
- The "malignant outliers" (terrorists / pedophiles) better-not-to-exist musing is **out of scope** — not argued in the library.
- **Right-to-die and transgenderism are optionality-only.**

This governs fork-1 framing copy when the library-layer is authored, and all future node authoring.

## Firewall rule — suffering-reduction may rebut, never recommend (added 2026-06-24, library-Claude)

The suite's motive is suffering-reduction (see *Umbrella*). This rule fixes how that motive may and may not enter a node, so the motive never hardens into a premise.

**Asymmetry-independence (binding on all node authoring).** Nodes argue **only** from sovereignty, consent, or role. They do **not** invoke Benatar's value-asymmetry — the pleasure/pain asymmetry — as a rebuttal premise. The harm-reductionist / negative-utilitarian convergence (right-to-die, abortion, trans care, and veganism each lower suffering by some route) is acknowledged here, at charter surface, as authorial **motivation**. It is never a warrant inside a node.

**REBUT, never RECOMMEND.** The operative boundary:

- **Permitted (defensive).** Suffering-reduction may *neutralize* an opponent's premise — e.g. dismantling the assumption that continued life is costless, as the coercion and palliative-care-sufficiency nodes already do. Here suffering is invoked to deny the objector a free move, not to ground a positive claim.
- **Prohibited (affirmative).** No node may argue "this reduces suffering, therefore the choice is good / better / recommended." That is a value-ranking of lives, and the suite does not rank lives — it defends a right against the people who would override it.

**Why this is load-bearing, not fastidiousness.** An affirmative harm-reduction framing inside Right-to-Die would put the library on record that less-of-this-kind-of-life is better — which is *precisely the expressivist objection's content*. Affirm it and you make that objection true at charter level, spend the expressivist node to buy the linkage, and re-import the asymmetry the whole suite is built to run without. The firewall is what keeps asymmetry-independence real rather than nominal.

**Lone licensed positive.** Abortion alone carries a positive but strictly advisory claim (per the *Register / scope invariant*). Every other node is optionality-only: it defeats objections to the right; it does not exhort the act.

**Three sharpenings (banked).**

1. **Veganism is other-regarding** — the patient is the animal, not the chooser. Its core is therefore a first-order *harm* claim, not an optionality claim, and it cannot be optionality-defended without contaminating this firewall; it argues like the flagship (harm + consent), not like Right-to-Die (sovereignty). *(Note: the sharpening as relayed tags veganism to the Anthropocentrism wing; this sits in tension with its current Wing-1 placement in the strand-map. Flagged for the structural ruling — not resolved here.)*
2. **Trans care stays sovereignty-framed** — "yours to determine," never suffering/dysphoria-framed. The dysphoria frame is both weaker on the merits and pathologizing. Same lesson as *anti-conscription > self-ownership*: the autonomy frame beats the value frame whenever the question is forced.
3. **Crossover is at the diagnosis, not the spine.** Right-to-Die's spine is sovereignty; antinatalism's is harm-asymmetry + consent-impossibility. The two converge at exactly one point — *selective precaution as a smuggled value* — and nowhere else. The shared front door (the orientation note) states a diagnosis the two libraries share; it is not a shared premise either library leans on.
